        Privacy

        Without an account, your saved bets and your state stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us.

        With an account, we store your username, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself) and the bets you save, so they sync across your devices. We don't collect your email, name or payment details, and we don't sell or share anything.

        To grade bets, your browser loads public final scores from ESPN. Odds come from The Odds API and Kalshi.

        You can delete your account and everything in it any time from My bets.
