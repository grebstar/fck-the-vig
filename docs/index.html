        Privacy

        Without an account, your state and My books stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us. Saving bets needs a free account.

        With an account, we store your username, your email, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself) and the bets you save, so they sync across your devices. Your email is only used to send password reset links and alerts you turn on. We don't send marketing, and we don't sell or share anything. We count how many bets you save each day for the free plan's limit.

        If you go Unlimited, Stripe handles the payment. Your card details go to Stripe, never to us. We keep the Stripe customer and subscription IDs, the plan's status and when it renews. If you used a code, we keep which code you used.

        For line-move alerts, we store what each alert is watching and, if you turn on push, a push address for each device you allow. It only works for sending you notifications. Turning push off or signing out removes it.

        My books is the list of books you picked. It stays in this browser, and in your account if you're signed in. We never connect to or see your sportsbook accounts.

        Shared links only contain the bets in the parlay, nothing about you.

        To grade bets, your browser loads public final scores from ESPN. Odds come from The Odds API and Kalshi.

        You can delete your account and everything in it any time from My bets.
